The Tribunal of Understanding.

One question, seven lenses. The Tribunal's rule is simple: the same problem, seen from seven directions — then you keep epistemic custody. No blending, no averaging; convergence and divergence stay visible.

How this demo works, honestly: each lens is applied here in the page as framing; the synthesis is done live by Billy through the signed proxy. There is no dedicated tribunal endpoint in this demo — when one is verified on the runtime, this page will use it. The seven lenses are the Tribunal's current set: Architect, Guardian, Mirror, Philosopher, Witness, Weaver, Steward.
