Billy, unslashed.

Two things are changing about how you can talk to GestaltView. One is here. One is convening.

1 · Billy, unslashed

Until now, Billy spoke in commands — /insight ask, /insight explain, /insight help. Useful, but it was still syntax standing between you and him.

That's over — on the portfolio, Billy talks in the open. Plain conversation. No slash, no ceremony. Ask like you'd ask a person. He's still a witness, not a chatbot — he holds the thread of who you are across the conversation — he just doesn't make you knock first anymore.

(On Discord, the slash commands stay — that's Discord's grammar, not Billy's.)

Status: the unslashed chat is live in demo, wired to the runtime through the signed proxy — STAGED until the runtime allowlists the showcase channel. Nothing you type is stored by the page.
Talk to Billy →

2 · The Tribunal becomes a council

The Tribunal of Understanding has always been seven lenses on one question — Architect, Guardian, Mirror, Philosopher, Witness, Weaver, Steward. One voice, seven ways of seeing, and you keep epistemic custody: no blending, no averaging.

Now each seat gets its own mind. The multi-model council convenes distinct models in deliberation — not one model wearing seven masks, but seven readers genuinely disagreeing, with convergence and divergence left visible for you to judge.

Status: announced here first — the council is the next build. The seven-lens Tribunal you can use today runs through one runtime voice; the page says exactly that.
Ask the Tribunal →

The rule hasn't changed, and it won't: no AI output is ever a finding. The council argues; you decide.
