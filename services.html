FAQ

Asked plainly, answered plainly.

What does "fixed scope" actually include?

Every engagement starts with a written proposal: outcome, deliverables, exclusions, timeline, price, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria — agreed before work begins. If it isn't written down, it isn't in scope.

Who owns the work when it's done?

You do. Every engagement ends with documentation, operating guidance, known limitations, and a walkthrough for whoever owns it next. Useful systems leave you more capable — not dependent.

How do revisions work?

Creative work (logos, decks) includes structured revision rounds with multiple concepts up front. For builds and advisory, the acceptance criteria define "done" — anything beyond the agreed scope gets re-scoped in writing before it starts, never mid-stream by surprise.

What does the handoff look like?

Documentation, operating guidance, known limitations, and a walkthrough with the next owner. Uncertainty is surfaced early in the engagement — never buried in the handoff.

How long do engagements take?

Opportunity audits run 1–2 weeks. Bespoke builds typically run 3–8+ weeks depending on scope. Fractional advisory is month-to-month. Operations engagements are scoped per situation after a discovery call.

Do you work part-time / contract / fractional?

Yes — that's the model. Advisory, build, design, and operations engagements are all structured as bounded contracts, and I'm set up for part-time and fractional arrangements.
