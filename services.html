How it works

Clarity before complexity

  1. Discovery call

    We confirm the business problem, urgency, owner, data, constraints — and whether there's a real fit. Bring the messy version.

  2. Fixed-scope proposal

    Outcome, deliverables, exclusions, timeline, price, responsibilities, acceptance criteria — written down before work starts.

  3. Build or advise

    Work moves through visible milestones. Uncertainty is surfaced early, not buried in the handoff.

  4. Handoff

    Documentation, operating guidance, known limitations, and a walkthrough. Useful systems leave you more capable — not dependent.
